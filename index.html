    Walk
    Tap a neighboring tile, or any tile you already cleared and the soldier walks there.
    Numbers
    They show how many mines are in the 8 tiles around that one.
    Flag
    Right click, press and hold, or use the Flag button. The soldier won't step on flags.
    Detector
    Marks every mine within 2 tiles of the soldier.
    Drone
    Pick an area and scan a 3×3 patch from the air.
    Tank
    Drives in a straight line and detonates the first mine. Press R or 3, then a direction.
    Shield
    Absorbs one blast. It triggers on its own.
    Map
    Missions 1 to 4 are forest, 5 to 8 desert, 9 to 12 night. The rules stay the same.
    Stars
    Reach the exit, recover the briefcases, and clear the mission challenge. They raise your rank.
    Medals
    Earned for feats. They sit at the top left. Tap one to see how to earn it.
    If you blow up
    A green light marks the tile you could have known was safe.
    Record
    Each mission stores your best time.
    Keyboard
    Arrows or WASD to walk. Q or 1 detector, E or 2 drone, R or 3 tank, F flag.
    Ads
    If you step on a mine, you can watch an ad and continue once per mission.
